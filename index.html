Essential security practices to keep your data safe online.

Core Security Best Practices

1. Multi-Factor Auth (MFA)

Always enable MFA on your accounts. It adds a secondary verification layer (like an authenticator app) even if your password is compromised.

2. Phishing Awareness

Be cautious with unexpected emails or links asking for sensitive credentials. Always verify the sender's domain address.

3. Patching & Updates

Keep your operating systems, browsers, and applications updated to protect against publicly known vulnerabilities.
